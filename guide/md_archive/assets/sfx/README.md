# 🎵 Sound Effects Assets

## ⚠️ Sound Files Required

The SFX system is configured but **needs actual sound files** to work.

---

## 📥 Where to Get Free Sound Files

### **Recommended Sources:**

1. **Zapsplat** - https://www.zapsplat.com/
   - Free with attribution
   - Filter: "UI Sounds", Duration < 1 second
   - Download as MP3

2. **Pixabay** - https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/
   - Completely free, no attribution
   - Search: "button click", "beep", "chime"
   - Download and rename to match filenames below

3. **Freesound** - https://freesound.org/
   - Free with various licenses (check CC0)
   - High quality
   - Search by tags: ui, button, success, error

## 🎨 Sound Specifications

- **Format:** MP3 or WAV
- **Bitrate:** 128kbps (keeps size small)
- **Duration:** 40-250ms (micro-sounds only)
- **Volume:** Normalized (not too loud)
- **Style:** Soft, neutral, non-distracting

---

## ⚡ Quick Method

1. Go to https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/button/
2. Download ~10 short click/beep sounds
3. Rename them to match the filenames above
4. Place in appropriate folders
5. Test in app!

---

## 🔧 Until You Add Sounds

The app will work fine but be **silent**. The SFX system checks if files exist before playing.

No errors will occur - sounds just won't play.

---

## ✅ Verification

After adding files, verify in app:
1. Go to Settings → Sound Effects
2. Tap any preview button
3. Should hear the sound!
