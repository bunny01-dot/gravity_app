## 💡 Quick Comparison: All 4 Past Tenses

| Tense | Formula | Use | Example |
|-------|---------|-----|---------|
| Simple Past | V2 | Finished action | "He studied" |
| Past Continuous | was/were + V-ing | Ongoing in past | "He was studying" |
| Past Perfect | had + V3 | Before another past | "He had studied" |
| Past Perfect Cont. | had been + V-ing | Duration until past | "He had been studying for 3 hours" |
