Privacy Policy for Gravity App

Last Updated: January 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Gravity App (English Learning App). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Email Address: Collected during registration via Firebase Authentication (email/password or Google Sign-In)
  • Name: Provided during signup or obtained from your Google account
  • Profile Photo: Optional, obtained from your Google account if you sign in with Google
  • Learning Progress: Your quiz scores, completed lessons, and mastery progress

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, and app version
  • Usage Data: App features used, time spent, and interaction patterns
  • FCM Token: For push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide personalized English learning experience
  • Track and save your learning progress across devices
  • Send educational notifications and announcements
  • Improve app features and user experience
  • Enable teacher-student interaction (for classroom features)

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using:

  • Firebase Firestore: For user profiles and learning progress
  • Firebase Storage: For uploaded resources (if applicable)
  • Local Device Storage: For offline access and caching

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls.

5. Data Sharing

We DO NOT sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Limited data may be shared with:

  • Firebase (Google): Our authentication and database provider
  • Teachers: If you join a classroom, your progress may be visible to assigned teachers

6. Push Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send:

  • Educational reminders and daily word notifications
  • Announcements from teachers (if enrolled in a class)
  • App updates and feature alerts

You can disable notifications in your device settings at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request complete account and data deletion
  • Portability: Export your learning progress data

9. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, your data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notifications or email.
